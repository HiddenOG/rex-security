Bag Checks

Suspect Detainment

Under Texas Code of Criminal Procedure Art. 14.01, a guard can detain a suspect — the same right any citizen has witnessing a felony.

Crime Deterrent

Security services help deter crime. The presence of a mobile patrol vehicle or field officer is a sign to criminals that the asset in place is secured.

Trespassing

Texas Penal Code §9.41 allows reasonable, non-deadly force to stop a trespass. Officers are trained to tell misdemeanor trespassing apart from criminal trespassing.
